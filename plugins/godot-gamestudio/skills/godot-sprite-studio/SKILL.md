# Godot Sprite Studio

1. Establish or read the style bible: dimensions, palette, perspective, outline, light direction, anchors, and naming.
2. Use an available image-generation capability for raster creation and deterministic image-processing scripts for extraction and cleanup.
3. Generate one coherent action family at a time. Keep detached effects separate when their geometry differs.
4. Inspect every frame for edge contact, scale drift, anchor drift, silhouette loss, transparency, and palette inconsistency.
5. Integrate assets into imports, SpriteFrames, AnimationPlayer, TileSet, or scenes as appropriate.
6. Produce a contact sheet or runtime capture as review evidence.

Do not treat a concept image as a finished playable asset.
