# Code Of Conduct

Godot Gamestudio welcomes people building games, agent workflows, benchmarks, documentation, and verification tools at every experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique artifacts and claims rather than people.
- Distinguish reproduced failures from suspicions and preferences.
- Disclose benchmark limitations, conflicts of interest, and unavailable verification.
- Protect private projects, credentials, personal information, and unpublished game assets.
- Give attribution for substantial ideas, code, fixtures, and research.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, publishing private information, benchmark manipulation, fabricated evidence, and knowingly unsafe instructions are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when behavior harms the project or its community. For a private report, open a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/schmoenraad/godot-gamestudio/security/advisories/new) titled `Code of Conduct report`. Include links and evidence, but do not repeat sensitive material unnecessarily.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.
